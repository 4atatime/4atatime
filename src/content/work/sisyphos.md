We started this collective from a charity fundraiser event in Dec 2024. Since then we have been organising social work events, as well as cultural activities like book clubs, both online and on-site. I work mainly as the designer here :)

The first fundraiser raised money for Roots, an organisation that supports refugees in transit near Dunkirk with essential services: internet access, hot showers, distribution of basic supplies, and food.
